## 中文版

终于不用为了强度放弃喜欢的步行手杖皮肤啦！

本作是 **H- 强化系列**模组之一，只对饥荒里的**原物品（步行手杖）**做修改，没有新增任何独立物品或地图实体。因此 **增删本 Mod 一般不会炸档**，可以放心使用；所有功能都有配置项可单独开关，按自己的喜好搭配即可。

我开发这个 Mod 的初衷很简单：对象玩饥荒种完田就不想玩了，因为是战斗苦手。现在有了这根手杖，她肯自己下地洞战斗了；而我这个负责帮忙锄地的，也就有了各种工具上的需求——于是有了这个 Mod。

### 功能一览

**⚔️ 战斗强化**
- 移速、伤害、位面伤害自由调节，还有「越战越强」的成长机制
- 远程 + 范围攻击，掌掴击飞、受击闪烁
- 吸血：造成的伤害按比例转化为生命 / 饥饿 / 理智
- 默认伤害适中（不算夸张），按需在配置里调整

**💡 光与生存**
- 发光、呼吸灯、粒子特效，天黑与装备状态一目了然
- 作祟复活（类似重生护符）、防 Boss 拍落、防偷窃
- 防雷、防雨、恒温

**🔧 多功能工具**
- 斧 / 镐 / 铲 / 锤 / 捕虫网 / 挖矿，一把手杖全搞定
- 右键开关即可切换多功能状态，装备名与光效同步提示

**🌾 自动化**
- 自动采摘（草、树枝、浆果等，范围可调）
- 自动耕地，还能在格子里放种子自动播种，同种种子无限堆叠

**💎 六色宝石轮盘（重点）**
装备手杖后，鼠标移入手杖格展开 7 项轮盘，左键切换：
- 🔴 红：点燃（火弹）
- 🔵 蓝：冰冻（冰弹，冻结强度高于官方冰魔杖）+ 右键扑灭燃烧物
- 🟢 绿：拆解（总开关打开时生效）
- 🟣 紫：传送 / 落雷
- 🟠 橙：闪烁（原生法杖传送）
- 🟡 黄：照明
- ⚪ 无宝石：切换总开关

宝石需点击解锁：持有对应颜色的**宝石 / 月眼 / 护符 / 法杖**任一即可解锁；右键或轮盘开关手杖时也会自动扫描解锁。持有**彩虹宝石、虹彩法杖或不朽宝石**则**直接解锁全部六色**。解锁状态跟随手杖保存，换人持有依然有效。

**🔍 15 格扫描**
手杖会扫描物品栏第 15 格及装备栏的物品，生成对应效果：复制物品、全科技、隐身、四季 Boss 特殊掉落物（巨鹿眼球 / 龙蝇皮 / 麋鹿鹅羽毛 / 熊皮）等，欢迎自行探索。

**🦷 合成与掉落**
- 海象牙不再需要科技即可制作
- 海象额外掉落海象牙

## English Version

Finally, you no longer have to give up your favorite walking cane skin for stats!

This is part of the **H-Enhancement** series. It only modifies the **vanilla item (walking cane)** and adds no new standalone items or world entities. Because of that, **adding/removing this mod is generally safe for your save** — no worries. Every feature has its own config option, so you can tune it to your taste.

I made this mod for a simple reason: my partner would stop playing after finishing the farm, since she's not good at combat. Now with this cane she's willing to go into the caves and fight herself — and I, the guy who does the tilling, needed a good all-in-one tool. Hence this mod.

### Features

**Combat**
- Adjustable movement speed, damage, planar damage, and a "stronger the longer you fight" scaling bonus
- Ranged + area attacks, knockback on hit, hit flash
- Lifesteal: convert a share of damage dealt into health / hunger / sanity
- Balanced default damage — tweak it in the settings if you want more

**Light & Survival**
- Glowing, breathing light, particle effects — night and toggle states at a glance
- Haunt resurrection (like a Life Giving Amulet), boss-knockback immunity, theft protection
- Lightning-proof, rain-proof, constant temperature

**Multi-Tool**
- Axe / pickaxe / shovel / hammer / bug net / mining — one cane does it all
- Right-click to toggle; the item name and light effect reflect the state

**Automation**
- Auto-harvest (grass, saplings, berries, etc., adjustable radius)
- Auto-farm, with optional seeds in a slot for automatic replanting (same seed stacks infinitely)

**Six-Gem Dial (highlight)**
Equip the cane, hover over its slot to expand a 7-option dial, left-click to switch:
- Red: ignite (fire projectile)
- Blue: freeze (ice projectile, stronger than the vanilla Ice Staff 3) + right-click to extinguish burning things
- Green: dismantle (only while the main toggle is on)
- Purple: teleport / lightning strike
- Orange: blink (vanilla staff teleport)
- Yellow: light
- None: toggle the main switch

Gems unlock by clicking: hold any matching-color **gem / mooneye / amulet / staff** to unlock it. Toggling the cane also auto-scans for unlockables. Holding an **Iridescent Gem, opalstaff (Iridescent Staff), or Immortal Gem** unlocks **all six at once**. Unlock states are saved on the cane itself, so they persist when it changes hands.

**15-Slot Scan**
The cane scans slot 15 of your inventory plus equipped items for effects: item duplication, all recipes unlocked, stealth, and seasonal Boss drops (Deerclops eyeball / Dragonfly scale / Moose/Goose feather / Bearger fur) — explore to find more.

**Crafting & Drops**
- Walrus Tusk craftable without a science machine
- Walrus extra tusk drop
